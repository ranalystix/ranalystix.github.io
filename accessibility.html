Accessibility statement

We want Ranalystix to be usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology. We are working towards conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA and review new screens against them; the figures shown in charts are also available as tables and as Excel exports.

Some parts of the application may not yet fully meet the guidelines. If you have difficulty using any part of it, write to support@ranalystix.com with the subject "Accessibility" and tell us what you were trying to do. We will respond within five business days and provide the information or functionality you need in an accessible way, including by email or a call.

Version US-2026-10-25.5.

This website was last reviewed on 25 October 2026 against WCAG 2.1 level AA: an automated check of every page, keyboard-only navigation with a visible focus indicator, a skip-to-content link, zoom to 200% and a 320-pixel-wide screen without loss of content, increased text spacing, and the page structure a screen reader announces (headings, landmarks, link names and image text).
